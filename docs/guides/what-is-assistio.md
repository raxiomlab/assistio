# What is AssistIO?

AssistIO is a **private, local-first AI workspace**. Instead of a chatbot that only
types text back, you get an assistant that has real, supervised access to your
computer and your projects: it reads and edits files, runs commands, searches the
web, manages Kanban boards and keeps working in the background after you step away.

> Give it an objective. It plans the work, uses tools, checks the result and reports
> back. You stay in control — every sensitive action asks for your approval first.

## The idea in one line

**Stop prompting. Start delegating.**

You describe the outcome you want. AssistIO figures out the steps, executes them with
real tools, and shows you what changed before it commits anything.

## What makes it different

| A typical chat assistant | AssistIO |
|---|---|
| Runs in someone else's cloud | Runs on your own machine |
| Can only use text you paste | Touches your real files and commands |
| Stops when you close the tab | Keeps going in the background |
| Forgets between conversations | Remembers, with scoped, searchable memory |
| All-or-nothing actions | Ask-first approvals, per tool |

## Who it's for

- **Developers** who want an assistant that actually touches the codebase.
- **Researchers and analysts** who need long-running work done in the background.
- **Anyone** who wants their AI tools, memory and history to stay on their own machine.

## What it is not

- It is **not a model provider.** You bring your own AI provider account and key —
  AssistIO connects to the provider you already use.
- It is **not a hosted service.** Your data lives on your machine, not in a cloud
  you do not control.
